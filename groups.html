What are Rock Network Groups?

Rock Network groups are groups of three or more that gather, grow, give and go make disciples.

Church Groups

This group:

  • Consists of believers
  • Is ready to gather, grow, give and go together
  • Agrees to meet long term
  • Uses the Jesus Disciple App

Discipleship Groups

This group:

  • Has someone who believes in Jesus leading others who do not
  • Agrees to meet together for a set period
  • Uses the Jesus Disciple App
