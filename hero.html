How we got started

  1. Where it began

    One local church

    A discipleship system for one local church designed to build solid lives.

  2. Growing

    Disciples became leaders

    As people were discipled, they started leading groups of their own, and those groups began to multiply.

  3. Multiplying

    Groups became churches

    Groups grew into new churches and local hubs, each one raising up leaders to send out.

  4. Today

    A global movement

    Today the network connects disciple makers across continents multiplying movements far beyond where it began.
